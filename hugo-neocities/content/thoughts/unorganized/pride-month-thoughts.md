Hey, happy Pride Month! Admittedly this month is kind of a weird topic for me because I've never really considered myself much of a member of the community. I don't have much interest in attending pride events, I don't really do anything relevant on social media or anything like that, but I like to consider myself generally supportive. I hesitate to use the term "ally" because IMO that's something you have to earn, and to be honest I don't really think I've done much of anything to earn it.

That said, I generally consider myself in some way both aromantic and asexual (though I struggle to apply labels to myself honestly). Without getting into too much detail I've always been at least mildly put off by sex, and I've never really had any feelings of romance, or a desire for it. I think that's part of why I don't really connect myself to the LGBT community mentally, because my only real connection is through things I don't feel, if that makes any sense. Like, sure, I've been attracted to characters of different genders so I guess I could call myself bi or poly, but I have much less attraction to actual people, so does that really count? I dunno, labels are weird to think about I guess.

Thinking back, I'm pretty sure the thing that helped me actively realize I was aromantic was the Sonic series{{< sup "down" 1 >}} in a kinda roundabout way. So, to preface, I'm super uninterested in the concept of shipping, and I generally don't care much one way or the other. That said, as a kid I always found myself a bit perturbed by media, particularly fan media, involving Sonic in romantic contexts. I think a lot of that was me unknowingly projecting my own aromantic feelings onto Sonic as a character, and so I always interpreted the dynamic between him and Amy as her failing to realize that he lacks the same romantic feelings that she has. So whenever I'd see fan media interpreting Sonic developing romantic feelings in return, I always felt bothered by the implication that someone without those feelings is simply one random incident away from becoming as romantic as everyone else, because that has never been the case for me. It's a bit embarassing to admit but I used to weirdly resent Amy as a character because of all that. I like to think that I've become at least a little bit more reasonable since then though, better at separating my own interpretations from others'.

Anyway, that's about all I really had to say. I don't have any super profound thoughts on the state of affairs here in America or abroad, other than to say that I'm continually dismayed to see the rights of people I care about constantly picked apart by governments that simultaneously offer all the protections they can to Nazis, pedophiles, and rapists if they're rich enough and/or the president, and I genuinely hope we'll see a day in our lifetimes when people no longer have to fear for their lives simply for the act of wanting to be their authentic selves.
